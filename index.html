Welcome,

—

I am Phinity. Here to adapt to your writing needs. Before we begin I just need to get to know you better to enhance the quality of our document production. I recommend you don't skip this part.

STEP 1 OF 5

Let's set your baseline.

PhinityX believes in continued improvement. A 2.5 CGPA student will not produce 4.2-level work overnight, and that is not a limitation; it is a foundation. Your output is calibrated to where you genuinely are, then aimed at where you want to be.

STEP 2 OF 5

Write to me.

PhinityX is not collecting personal data; it is studying how you write. Describe yourself, your thoughts, your worldview, anything that feels natural. Write at least five paragraphs. Do not use bullet points. Do not write what you think sounds good; write the way you actually think. Nothing about this is stored beyond your device.

STEP 3 OF 5

Who are you, in fiction?

Choose five fictional characters from film, television, books, anime, anywhere, that you feel represent who you are as a person. This is not about who you admire; it is about who you recognise in yourself. PhinityX uses these to understand your personality, not just your words. A Harvey Specter tells us something a personality quiz never could.
